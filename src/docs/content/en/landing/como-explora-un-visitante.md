# How visitors browse the site

A visitor is someone who arrives at OMZONE without being logged in — a potential client who found you through Google, social media, or word of mouth.

Understanding how they navigate helps you create better publications and experiences.

---

## The typical visitor journey

```
1. Visitor arrives at the site
   (via Google, Instagram, a friend's recommendation)
        ↓
2. Lands on the home page
   (first impression — "what is this place?")
        ↓
3. Browses the experience catalog
   ("what can I actually do here?")
        ↓
4. Opens an experience that catches their eye
   (reads the description, scrolls the photos, checks the price)
        ↓
5. Decides to book
   (clicks "Book now" or "Reserve")
        ↓
6. Prompted to log in or create an account
   (new visitors create one in about 2 minutes)
        ↓
7. Completes checkout
        ↓
8. Receives confirmation email and ticket
```

---

## Where visitors make decisions

### Do I stay or leave?

Visitors decide in the first few seconds whether to keep exploring. Your home page and experience photos carry that weight.

**What helps:** High-quality images, a clear value proposition ("Yoga retreats in Puerto Vallarta"), and a visible call to action.

---

### Am I interested in this experience?

When a visitor opens an experience page, they're asking:
- What exactly will I experience?
- Is this for someone like me?
- How much does it cost?
- When can I go?

**What helps:** A narrative description (not a bullet list of features), real photos, clear pricing, and visible upcoming dates.

---

### Do I trust this place?

Before paying, visitors want to feel confident they're in good hands.

**What helps:** Client testimonials, instructor bios, photos of the team and the space, and a professional, well-maintained site.

---

## Returning visitors

A visitor might save your site and come back later. That means it's important to:
- Keep schedules up to date.
- Publish new experiences regularly.
- Make sure pricing is always visible and correct.
