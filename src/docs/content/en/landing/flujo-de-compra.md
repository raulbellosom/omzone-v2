# The checkout flow

Here's the full journey from "I want this" to "I have my ticket."

---

## Step 1 — They choose the experience

The client is on an experience page and clicks the main action button: **Book now**, **Purchase**, or **Request** — depending on how the experience is set up.

---

## Step 2 — They pick a date and time (if applicable)

For experiences with available schedules, the client selects when they want to attend.

Only sessions with open spots appear as options.

---

## Step 3 — They choose a price tier (if there are multiple)

Some experiences offer different price levels:
- General admission
- Member price
- Early bird rate

The client selects the one that applies to them.

---

## Step 4 — They add extras (optional)

If the experience has add-ons available (e.g., a post-session massage, transport, a starter kit), the client can select them here.

---

## Step 5 — They log in or create an account

If the client isn't logged in, they're prompted to:
- **Log in** to an existing account, or
- **Create an account** with their name, email, and password.

Creating an account takes about two minutes.

---

## Step 6 — They review and pay

The client sees a summary of what they're buying:
- Experience name and details
- Date and time
- Itemized pricing
- Total

They enter their payment details and confirm.

> Payments are processed securely. No card data is stored in OMZONE.

---

## Step 7 — Confirmation and ticket

After a successful payment:
1. A confirmation screen appears.
2. A confirmation email is sent to their inbox.
3. The ticket with its QR code appears in their portal under **My tickets**.

---

## The request flow (private experiences)

Some experiences skip the direct purchase and use a **request** flow instead:

1. Client fills in the request form (preferred dates, group size, notes).
2. The OMZONE team receives the request in the admin panel.
3. The team replies with a confirmation or a custom quote.
4. If the client accepts, they receive a payment link.
5. After payment, the ticket is generated.

→ See **Booking requests** in the admin section for how to handle these from your side.
