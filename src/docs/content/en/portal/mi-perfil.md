# My profile

This is where you update your personal information and account settings.

---

## Getting there

From your portal, click **My profile** in the menu, or your name in the top corner of the screen.

---

## What you can update

- **Full name** — Your name as it appears on tickets and confirmation emails.
- **Phone number** — Your contact number.
- **Profile photo** — An optional avatar.

> Your **email address** is your login identifier and can't be changed from this screen. If you need to update it, contact the OMZONE team.

---

## Changing your password

1. In **My profile**, find the **Change password** or **Security** option.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Type your new password (at least 8 characters — include letters, numbers, and a symbol for best security).
4. Confirm the new password.
5. Click **Save**.

> If you've forgotten your current password, use **Forgot your password?** on the login screen instead.

---

## Saving your changes

Always click **Save** after editing your details. Changes aren't applied until you confirm.

---

## Communication preferences

If the platform supports it, you can also manage whether you'd like to receive reminder emails and notifications.
