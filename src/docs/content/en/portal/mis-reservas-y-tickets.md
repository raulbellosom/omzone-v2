# My tickets & reservations

This is where you'll find all your tickets — both upcoming and past.

---

## Getting there

From your portal, click **My tickets** in the menu.

---

## What is a ticket?

A **ticket** is your digital proof of reservation. It has a unique QR code that you present on the day of the experience so the OMZONE team can check you in.

---

## The ticket list

Your tickets are sorted with the soonest upcoming ones at the top. Each ticket shows:

- The name of the experience.
- The date and time.
- The venue.
- The ticket status.

---

## Ticket statuses

| Status    | What it means                                             |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active    | Your ticket is valid — use it the day of the experience   |
| Used      | You attended — your ticket was scanned                    |
| Cancelled | Your reservation was cancelled                            |
| Expired   | The session date passed without the ticket being used     |

---

## Viewing your QR code

1. Click the ticket you want to open.
2. The QR code appears on screen.
3. Show it to the OMZONE staff on arrival — they'll scan it.

> No need to print it. Showing it on your phone is perfectly fine.

---

## Downloading your ticket

If you want to save a copy:

1. Open the ticket.
2. Tap **Download** (if available).
3. It saves as an image to your device.

---

## Lost your ticket?

Don't worry. As long as your account exists, your tickets do too. Just log in, go to **My tickets**, and open the one you need.

If you're having trouble finding it, the OMZONE team can look it up for you using your name and email.

---

## Reservations without a ticket

Some experiences don't generate a ticket — instead, you receive a **booking confirmation**. This is normal for experiences that don't require QR check-in.
