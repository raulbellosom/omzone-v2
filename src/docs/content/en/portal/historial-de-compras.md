# Purchase history

Your purchase history is a complete record of every order you've made through OMZONE.

---

## Getting there

From your portal, go to **My dashboard** or look for **My purchases** / **History** in the menu.

---

## What the history shows

Each entry includes:

- **Experience name** — What you purchased.
- **Purchase date** — When you paid.
- **Amount paid** — What you were charged.
- **Status** — Whether the order is active, completed, or cancelled.
- **Link to tickets** — Jump straight to the tickets for that order.

---

## Filtering your history

You can filter by:

- Time period (this month, last 3 months, etc.).
- Order status.

---

## Downloading a receipt

From any order's detail view, you can download a payment receipt for your personal records.

---

## Missing a purchase?

It might not appear if:

- The order is in **Pending** status (payment wasn't completed).
- You used a different email address for that purchase.

If you think there's an error, contact the OMZONE team with the confirmation number from your email.
