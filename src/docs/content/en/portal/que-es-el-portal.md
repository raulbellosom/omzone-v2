# What is the client portal?

The **client portal** is your personal space on OMZONE. It's different from the public site — this area is only for you, and you access it with your registered account.

---

## What the portal is for

From your portal you can:

- View your active tickets and upcoming reservations.
- Show or download the QR code for your tickets.
- Check your pass balance and expiry dates.
- Review your full purchase history.
- Update your personal details and password.

In short: it's where you manage everything you've purchased through OMZONE.

---

## Getting to the portal

1. Go to the OMZONE website.
2. Click **Log in**.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. You'll be taken directly to your portal.

> If you don't have an account yet, you can create one from the login screen.

---

## The portal menu

Once you're in, the menu has these sections:

| Section          | What it's for                                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Explore          | Browse experiences available to book                       |
| My dashboard     | A quick summary of your recent bookings and activity       |
| My tickets       | Active and past tickets                                    |
| My passes        | Your active passes and remaining balance                   |
| My profile       | Your personal details and password                         |

---

## Can I buy from the portal?

Yes. From the **Explore** section you can browse all available experiences and purchase new ones directly from your account.

---

## Can I access the admin panel?

No. The client portal is completely separate from the admin panel. As a client, you only see and manage what belongs to your own account.
