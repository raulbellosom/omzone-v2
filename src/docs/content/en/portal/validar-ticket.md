# Using your ticket on the day

The day has arrived. Here's exactly what to do to get checked in without a hitch.

---

## Before you leave home

1. **Confirm the time and location** — It's on your ticket and in your confirmation email.
2. **Open your ticket** — Log into the portal, go to **My tickets**, and open today's ticket. The QR code will be on screen.
3. **Charge your phone** — Make sure you have enough battery to show the QR.

---

## When you arrive

1. Head to the check-in desk or reception area.
2. When prompted, show the QR code on your phone to the OMZONE staff.
3. They'll scan it with their device.
4. You'll see a confirmation in seconds.
5. You're in. Enjoy!

---

## Opening your ticket quickly

1. Open the OMZONE portal (if you're already logged in, it's instant).
2. Go to **My tickets**.
3. Tap today's ticket.
4. The QR code appears immediately.

> **Tip:** Keep the portal open on your phone from the time you leave home so you're not scrambling to find the ticket at the door.

---

## If the QR code won't scan

It happens occasionally. Here's what to try:

- **Maximize your screen brightness** before presenting.
- **Hold the phone a bit farther away** from the scanner (around 15-20 cm / 6-8 inches works well).
- If it still doesn't scan, **give the staff your name and email** — they can look you up in the system and check you in manually.

---

## If you forgot your phone

Not a problem. Staff can find you by your name or email in the system and verify your ticket without the QR code.

---

## Day-of FAQs

**Do I need to print my ticket?**
No. The QR code on your phone screen is all you need.

**Can someone else use my ticket?**
Tickets are personal. Each attendee needs their own ticket.

**What if I'm running late?**
It depends on the experience's late arrival policy. If you're going to be late, contact OMZONE beforehand.

**What can staff see when they scan my QR?**
Only the information needed to check you in: your name and whether your ticket is valid.
