# Launch a new session

This guide walks you through everything you need to do to take a new session from idea to live on the site.

**Time to complete:** About 20–30 minutes.

---

## What you need before you start

- The session name and a short description (2-3 lines).
- At least one photo.
- Pricing information.
- An instructor or facilitator assigned as a resource.
- At least one scheduled date.

---

## Step 1 — Create the experience

1. Go to **Experiences** in the admin panel.
2. Click **New experience**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Public name** — What clients will see.
   - **Internal name** — For your reference (can be the same).
   - **Experience type** — Select *Session*.
   - **Short description** — 2-3 lines that hook the reader.
   - **Full description** — The in-depth narrative.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## Step 2 — Set pricing

1. Open the experience and go to the **Pricing** tab.
2. Click **Add price tier**.
3. Set the price, name (e.g., "General"), and whether it's active.
4. Save the tier.

Add more tiers if you have different price levels (e.g., members, early bird).

---

## Step 3 — Schedule the first session

1. Go to **Agenda** in the panel (or open the experience's **Schedules** tab).
2. Click **New schedule** or **Add date**.
3. Set the date, time, duration, and capacity.
4. Assign the instructor (resource).
5. Assign the location.
6. Save.

---

## Step 4 — Create the publication

1. Go to **Publications**.
2. Click **New publication**.
3. Link it to your new experience.
4. Upload the cover image.
5. Build out the editorial sections: text blocks, photo galleries, instructor bio.
6. Once ready, set the publication to **Active**.

---

## Step 5 — Activate the experience

1. Go back to the experience.
2. Set the status to **Active**.
3. Save.

The experience is now live on the public site.

---

## Step 6 — Verify on the site

Open your public site and confirm:
- The experience appears in the catalog.
- The experience page loads correctly (description, photos, pricing).
- The date you scheduled is shown and bookable.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Experience created with name, description, and type
- [ ] At least one active price tier
- [ ] At least one scheduled date
- [ ] Publication created, linked, and active
- [ ] Experience set to Active
- [ ] Verified on the public site
