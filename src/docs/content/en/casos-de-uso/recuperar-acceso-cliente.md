# Recover a client's access

One of the most common support situations: a client can't get into their account. Here's how to handle every scenario.

---

## Scenario 1 — "I forgot my password"

**Tell the client:**

1. Go to the OMZONE login page.
2. Click **Forgot your password?**
3. Enter the email address used to create the account.
4. Check their inbox for a password reset email.
5. Follow the link in the email to set a new password.

**If the email doesn't arrive:** See Scenario 2.

---

## Scenario 2 — "I didn't receive the reset email"

**Things to check:**

- **Spam/junk folder** — The email sometimes ends up there.
- **Correct email** — Are they sure they're using the right address? If they've used multiple emails over the years, they might be checking the wrong inbox.
- **Wait a few minutes** — Occasionally emails are slightly delayed.

**If the email is confirmed but still not arriving:**

1. Go to **Clients** in the admin panel.
2. Search for the client's account by email.
3. From the client's profile, you can trigger a password reset or a new confirmation email from your side.

---

## Scenario 3 — "My account seems to be blocked"

1. Go to **Clients** in the admin panel.
2. Find the client and open their profile.
3. Check the account status — if it's disabled or blocked, you can re-enable it from there.
4. Let the client know and ask them to try logging in again.

---

## Scenario 4 — "I don't think I have an account"

It's possible the client made a purchase without realizing they created an account, or their account is under a different email.

**Steps:**

1. Go to **Clients** in the admin panel.
2. Search by their name, email, or phone number.
3. If an account exists, let them know which email to use.
4. If no account exists, they can create one on the login page by clicking **Sign up**.

---

## Scenario 5 — "I want to change my email address"

Clients can't change their email themselves (it's their login identifier). To change it:

1. Go to **Clients** in the admin panel.
2. Find the client.
3. Update the email from the client's profile (if your role allows it).
4. Let the client know the new email is now their login.

> Note: Any orders and tickets already associated with the old email will still appear in the account history.

---

## Quick reference

| Situation                          | Action                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Forgot password                    | Use "Forgot password?" on login page               |
| Reset email not arriving           | Check spam; admin can trigger reset manually        |
| Account blocked                    | Admin re-enables from Clients panel                 |
| Doesn't know which email was used  | Admin searches by name/phone in Clients panel       |
| Wants to change email              | Admin updates email from client's profile           |
