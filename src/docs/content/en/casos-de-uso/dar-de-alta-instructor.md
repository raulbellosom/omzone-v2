# Add a new instructor

When a new instructor or facilitator joins your team, follow these steps to get them set up in the system and featured on your site.

---

## Step 1 — Create them as a resource

In OMZONE, instructors are managed as **resources** — things that can be assigned to scheduled sessions.

1. Go to **Resources** in the admin panel.
2. Click **New resource**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — The instructor's full name.
   - **Type** — Select *Instructor* (or the relevant category).
   - **Bio** — A short paragraph about their background and style.
   - **Photo** — Upload a professional headshot.
4. Save.

---

## Step 2 — Assign them to sessions

Now that they're in the system, you can assign them to scheduled sessions.

1. Go to **Agenda** and open or create a schedule.
2. In the **Resources** field, select the instructor.
3. Save.

The instructor is now associated with those sessions. Their name can show up on the experience page.

---

## Step 3 — Feature them on the site

To give the instructor a profile on the public site:

1. Go to **Publications** and open the publication linked to their featured experience(s).
2. Add or edit an **Instructor** section.
3. Include their name, photo, and bio.
4. Save and confirm the publication is still **Active**.

---

## What the instructor can see

This step is for their **admin or operator access** — only relevant if the instructor will also use the admin panel.

1. Go to **Clients** → search by email or create a new account.
2. Once the account exists, assign the appropriate role from **Team** (if applicable).
3. They can log in and see the sections relevant to their role.

> Instructors who only teach don't need admin access. They're simply assigned to schedules as resources.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Resource created with name, type, bio, and photo
- [ ] Assigned to at least one scheduled session
- [ ] Featured in a publication (if they should appear publicly)
- [ ] Admin account created (only if needed)
