# Organize a weekend retreat

Retreats are multi-day experiences that require a bit more planning than a single session, but the process follows the same structure.

**Time to complete:** About 45–60 minutes (spread across planning and setup).

---

## Before you start: the planning checklist

Before touching the admin panel, confirm you have:

- [ ] Retreat name and concept
- [ ] Dates and schedule (day by day)
- [ ] Location confirmed (venue, address)
- [ ] What's included (meals, materials, accommodation if applicable)
- [ ] Pricing (total price per person, any different tiers)
- [ ] Instructor(s) or facilitators
- [ ] Visual content: cover image, gallery photos
- [ ] The narrative — the story you want to tell

---

## Step 1 — Create the retreat experience

1. Go to **Experiences** → **New experience**.
2. Set the type to *Retreat*.
3. Enter the public name, short description, and full description.
4. Save.

---

## Step 2 — Create the location (if new)

If the retreat is at a venue you haven't used before:

1. Go to **Locations** → **New location**.
2. Add the name, address, and any notes.
3. Save.

---

## Step 3 — Set pricing

1. Go to the experience's **Pricing** tab.
2. Add the price tier(s):
   - "Early bird" (if you're offering early registration)
   - "General"
   - "Accommodation included" (if applicable)
3. Activate the tiers you want visible.

---

## Step 4 — Schedule the retreat

1. Go to **Agenda** → **New schedule**.
2. Set the start date and end date.
3. Set the capacity (maximum attendees).
4. Assign the facilitators as resources.
5. Assign the location.
6. Save.

> For multi-day retreats, you typically create one schedule entry that spans the full date range.

---

## Step 5 — Build the publication

This is what makes a retreat sell. Put care into it.

1. Go to **Publications** → **New publication**.
2. Link it to the retreat experience.
3. Upload a striking cover image.
4. Build sections:
   - **What awaits you** — An evocative, sensory description of the experience.
   - **The schedule** — A day-by-day or session-by-session breakdown.
   - **What's included** — A clear list.
   - **Your guide / facilitator** — Bio and photo.
   - **Location** — Where it takes place, how to get there.
   - **FAQs** — What to bring, physical requirements, etc.
5. Set the publication to **Active**.

---

## Step 6 — Activate the experience

Set the experience status to **Active** and save.

---

## Step 7 — Verify

Check the public site:
- The retreat appears in the catalog.
- The page tells a compelling story.
- Pricing and capacity are visible.
- The registration button works.

---

## Promotion tips

- Publish 4-6 weeks before the retreat date to give people time to plan.
- Feature it on the home page if your platform supports it.
- Share the experience page URL directly on social media.
