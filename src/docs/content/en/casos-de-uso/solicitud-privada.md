# Handle a private event request

Some experiences — private yoga sessions, group events, bachelorette retreats — don't have a fixed price or a standard schedule. Instead, interested clients submit a **booking request** and your team responds with details or a custom quote.

---

## How the request flow works

```
Client submits a request form
        ↓
Request appears in admin panel
        ↓
Your team reviews it
        ↓
You decline, quote, or confirm
        ↓
If confirmed → client receives payment link
        ↓
After payment → ticket is generated
```

---

## Step 1 — Make sure the experience uses request mode

For experiences that should use this flow:

1. Go to **Experiences** and open the experience.
2. In the **Sale mode** field, select *By request*.
3. Save.

Clients will now see a "Request" button instead of "Book now."

---

## Step 2 — Client submits a request

The client fills in the form:
- Preferred date(s)
- Number of attendees
- Any specific needs or notes

They submit. You get a notification.

---

## Step 3 — Review the request

1. Go to **Booking requests** in the admin panel.
2. Click the request to open it.
3. Review the details: who's asking, when, how many people, their notes.

---

## Step 4 — Respond

From the request, you have three options:

### Accept and confirm
- Set the date, time, and final price.
- Add any notes for the client.
- Click **Confirm**.
- The client receives a payment link by email.

### Send a quote
- If you need to propose a custom price or clarify details first.
- Enter your quote and a message.
- Click **Send quote**.
- The client can review and accept.

### Decline
- If you can't accommodate the request.
- Add an optional message explaining why.
- Click **Decline**.
- The client is notified.

---

## Step 5 — Client pays

Once the client receives the payment link:
1. They open the link.
2. They review the details and pay.
3. Payment is processed.
4. A ticket with a QR code is generated automatically.
5. The ticket appears in their portal and is sent by email.

---

## Tracking requests

In **Booking requests**, you can filter by:
- Status (Pending, Confirmed, Declined, Paid)
- Date
- Experience

Use this view to stay on top of all incoming requests and follow up as needed.
