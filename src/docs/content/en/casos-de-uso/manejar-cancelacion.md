# Handle a cancellation

Cancellations happen. Here's how to handle the most common scenarios cleanly.

---

## Scenario 1 — A client wants to cancel their ticket

When a client asks to cancel their reservation:

1. Go to **Tickets** and find their ticket (search by name, email, or order number).
2. Open the ticket.
3. Review the cancellation policy that applies.
4. Click **Cancel ticket**.
5. Confirm.

The ticket is marked **Cancelled**. The client won't be able to check in with it.

**What about a refund?**
Refunds are handled separately through your payments settings. If a refund is due, process it from the **Orders** section.

---

## Scenario 2 — You need to cancel a specific order

If a client's entire order needs to be cancelled (not just one ticket):

1. Go to **Orders** and find the order.
2. Open it.
3. Click **Cancel order**.
4. Select the reason (if applicable).
5. Confirm.

All tickets in that order are cancelled automatically.

---

## Scenario 3 — You need to cancel an entire session

When circumstances require cancelling a whole scheduled session:

1. Go to **Agenda** and open the session.
2. Click **Cancel session** (or **Archive schedule**).
3. Confirm.

**After cancelling:**
- All tickets for that session are automatically marked as **Cancelled**.
- Affected clients should be notified. Use **Send notification** from the session or contact them manually.
- If refunds are applicable, process them through **Orders**.

---

## Scenario 4 — Rescheduling instead of cancelling

If you want to move a session to a different date rather than cancelling it:

1. **Cancel the original session** (or mark it as moved).
2. **Create a new schedule** with the updated date and time.
3. **Notify clients** — let them know the new date and how to rebook.

> Currently, tickets are tied to specific schedules. Clients need to rebook for the new date rather than having their ticket automatically transferred.

---

## Communicating with clients

When cancelling something, always let the client know:
- **What** was cancelled.
- **Why** (if appropriate).
- **What they should do** — rebook, wait for a refund, contact you.

Use **Send notification** from the relevant order or session, or reach out directly by email.
