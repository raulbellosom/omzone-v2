# End-of-day checklist

A quick daily routine that keeps your operations tight and tomorrow's sessions running smoothly.

---

## 1. Review today's sessions

Go to **Agenda** and look at today's schedule.

- Did all sessions run as planned?
- Were there any no-shows?
- Did anyone show up without a ticket? (If so, log them as an assisted sale.)

---

## 2. Mark attendance or close sessions

If your workflow includes marking sessions as completed:

1. Open each session from today's schedule.
2. Confirm it ran (or mark it as cancelled if it didn't).
3. Save.

---

## 3. Check pending orders

Go to **Orders** and filter by **Pending**.

- Are there any orders that haven't been paid?
- If a payment is stuck or overdue, look into it or reach out to the client.

---

## 4. Review pending booking requests

Go to **Booking requests** and look at anything that hasn't been responded to.

- Respond to any requests that came in today.
- Follow up on any quotes sent earlier that haven't been confirmed.

---

## 5. Check tomorrow's schedule

Go to **Agenda** and review tomorrow's sessions.

- Are all sessions staffed (resources assigned)?
- Are the locations set?
- Is capacity set correctly?
- Are there any sessions that look overfull or underfull?

---

## 6. Review new client signups (optional)

If you want to track growth, go to **Clients** and sort by newest.

- Did anyone new sign up today?
- Are there any new accounts that need attention?

---

## 7. Check for any site issues (quick scan)

Open the public site and do a 2-minute check:

- Do the featured experiences still look right?
- Are tomorrow's dates showing up correctly?
- Is anything obviously broken or missing?

---

## That's it

A full end-of-day review takes 10-15 minutes. Doing it consistently means you'll catch small problems before they become big ones.
