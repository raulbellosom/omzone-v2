# Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

---

## For clients

### How do I book an experience?

Browse the experiences on the site, click the one you want, choose a date and price tier, and follow the checkout steps. You'll need to create an account if you don't have one — it takes about 2 minutes.

### Do I need to print my ticket?

No. Just open your portal, tap **My tickets**, and show the QR code on your phone screen when you arrive.

### What if I lose my ticket?

It's in your portal — you can't lose it. Log in at any time, go to **My tickets**, and your ticket is right there.

### Can I cancel my reservation?

Cancellations depend on the experience's policy. Contact the OMZONE team through the site, and they'll help you based on your situation.

### How do passes work?

A pass gives you a set number of sessions (or unlimited access for a time period). When you book a compatible experience, you choose to use your pass instead of paying separately. Your balance updates automatically.

### Can I share my ticket with someone else?

No. Tickets are personal and tied to your account.

### What if I forget the email I used to sign up?

Contact the OMZONE team with your name and phone number — they can look up your account.

### How do I change my password?

Log in, go to **My profile**, and use the **Change password** option. If you've forgotten your current password, use **Forgot your password?** on the login screen.

## For administrators and operators

### How do I create a new experience?

Go to **Experiences** → **New experience**. Fill in the details, add pricing and schedules, create a publication, and set everything to Active. See [Launch a new session](/help/docs/en/casos-de-uso/lanzar-una-sesion) for a full walkthrough.

### Why isn't my experience appearing on the site?

The most common reasons: the experience isn't set to Active, there's no active price tier, or there's no active publication linked. Check all three. See [Common issues](/help/docs/en/referencia/problemas-frecuentes) for details.

### How do I issue a ticket manually?

Go to **Assisted sale** in the admin panel. Search for the client, select the experience and session, choose how they paid, and confirm. A ticket is generated immediately.

### Can I cancel a single ticket without cancelling the whole order?

Yes. Go to **Tickets**, find the specific ticket, and cancel it from the ticket's detail view. The rest of the order remains intact.

### How do I process a refund?

Refunds are handled from **Orders**. Open the relevant order and look for the refund option. The exact process depends on whether the original payment was made through the platform or another method.

### Can I give an operator access to the admin panel?

Yes. Go to **Clients**, find or create their account, and assign the **Operator** role. Operators have access to day-to-day management but not sensitive settings.

### What happens if I deactivate a price tier?

Existing tickets and orders already using that price are not affected. The price tier simply stops appearing for new bookings.

### How do I archive an experience that's no longer being offered?

Go to the experience and set its status to **Inactive** or use the archive option. It will stop showing on the public site but remain in your records.

### How do I reach the OMZONE support team?

Use the contact form on the public site, or reach out through the channel your team uses (email, WhatsApp, etc.).
