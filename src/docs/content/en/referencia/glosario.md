# Glossary

Quick-reference definitions for the terms you'll encounter while using OMZONE.

---

## Experience

The core unit of everything on OMZONE. An experience is a wellness activity you offer — a yoga class, a sound bath, a weekend retreat, a private session. It's the "product" clients browse and book.

---

## Edition

A variation of an experience with different characteristics — for example, the same yoga class offered in a morning format and an evening format. Editions let you present multiple versions without creating completely separate experiences.

---

## Schedule

A specific date and time when an experience is taking place. One experience can have many scheduled sessions. A schedule has a capacity (maximum attendees), an assigned location, and assigned resources (like instructors).

---

## Ticket

Digital proof that a client has a confirmed spot in a session. Each ticket has a unique QR code used for check-in. Tickets are stored in the client portal under **My tickets**.

---

## Reservation

The act of securing a spot in a specific session. When a client completes a purchase for a session, a reservation is created and a ticket is generated.

---

## Order

The transaction record of a client's purchase. An order can contain one or more tickets. It records what was bought, when, at what price, and the payment status.

---

## Pass

A credit-based or time-based membership that lets a client attend multiple sessions without buying each one separately. A session pass has a set number of uses; a time-based pass allows unlimited access within a defined period.

---

## Package

A bundle that includes multiple sessions or items purchased together in one transaction. When a client buys a package, they receive a pass with the corresponding credits or validity.

---

## Publication

The editorial layer of an experience — the story, photos, and formatted content that appears on the public site. A publication is linked to an experience but kept separate so you can update the narrative without changing the commercial details.

---

## Section

A content block within a publication (e.g., a text block, a photo gallery, an instructor bio, a FAQ list). Publications are built by combining sections.

---

## Add-on

An optional extra that clients can purchase alongside an experience — for example, a post-session massage, transport, a starter kit. Add-ons are linked to specific experiences and appear at checkout.

---

## Booking request

An inquiry submitted by a client for an experience that doesn't have fixed pricing or a public schedule (typically private events). The OMZONE team reviews the request and responds with a confirmation or a custom quote.

---

## Assisted sale

A purchase entered manually by an administrator or operator on behalf of a client — for example, when someone pays in person or by phone and you need to log it in the system.

---

## Client portal

The private section of the site accessible to clients after they log in. The portal is where clients find their tickets, passes, purchase history, and profile.

---

## Resource

Anything assignable to a scheduled session — an instructor, a therapist, a piece of equipment, a room. Resources help you manage availability and avoid double-booking.

---

## Location

A physical space where sessions take place. Locations are defined once and reused across schedules.
