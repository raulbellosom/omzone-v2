# Experiences

Experiences are the heart of OMZONE. Everything else — pricing, schedules, tickets, publications — connects back to an experience.

---

## Types of experiences

| Type              | What it is                                                        |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Session           | A recurring class or event (yoga, meditation, breathwork)         |
| Immersion         | A deeper, longer single-day experience with a specific focus      |
| Retreat           | Multi-day program, often with lodging included                    |
| Stay              | Extended residential experience                                   |
| Private Event     | Custom experience for a group — booked by request                 |

---

## Creating a new experience

1. Go to **Experiences** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New experience**.
3. Fill in the basic info:
   - **Internal name** — Your reference only. Clients never see this.
   - **Public name** — What clients see on the site.
   - **Type** — Session, Immersion, Retreat, Stay, or Private Event.
   - **Short description** — A 2-3 line summary that appears in listings.
   - **Full description** — The detailed description for the experience page.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## Experience status

| Status  | What it means                                        |
| ------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Draft   | Only visible to admins — not on the public site      |
| Active  | Visible on the public site, available to book        |
| Paused  | Hidden from the public temporarily                   |
| Archived| No longer active — kept for historical records       |

> An experience won't appear on the public site until its status is **Active** and it has at least one active price and one active publication.

---

## Sale mode

The **Sale mode** controls how clients book the experience:

- **Direct purchase** — Client selects a date/time and pays immediately.
- **Request** — Client fills in a request form. You review and confirm (or quote) before payment.
- **Pass only** — The experience can only be booked using a pass, not purchased individually.

---

## Editing an experience

Click any experience in the list to open it. From there you can update any field, change the status, or navigate to its pricing, schedule, add-ons, and linked publications.

---

## Deleting vs. archiving

Avoid deleting experiences that have orders or tickets attached to them — this can affect historical records. Use **Archive** instead to hide the experience without losing the data.
