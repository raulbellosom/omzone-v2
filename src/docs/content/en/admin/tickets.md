# Tickets

A **ticket** is the entry pass for a specific session. It's generated automatically when an order is completed, and contains a unique QR code that the client presents on the day of the experience.

---

## Finding a ticket

From **Orders**: open any order — its tickets are listed at the bottom.

From **Agenda**: open a schedule and see the full attendee list with one ticket per person.

Searching by client name or email also brings up their tickets.

---

## Ticket statuses

| Status  | What it means                                             |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Active  | Valid — the client can use it to attend                   |
| Used    | Already scanned — client attended the session             |
| Cancelled | The reservation was cancelled                           |
| Expired | The session date passed without the ticket being scanned  |

---

## Validating a ticket

On the day of the session, scan the client's QR code using the validation tool in the panel (or a mobile scanner). The ticket status changes from **Active** to **Used** immediately.

If the QR doesn't scan, look up the client by name or email and validate manually.

---

## Manually changing a ticket's status

If a ticket was scanned incorrectly or needs correction:
1. Open the ticket.
2. Click **Mark as used** (or the appropriate action).
3. The change is recorded in the ticket's activity log with a timestamp.

Use this carefully — ticket status changes are logged and visible.

---

## Cancelling a ticket

1. Open the ticket (or the order it belongs to).
2. Click **Cancel ticket**.
3. The client receives an email notification.
4. If a refund applies, process it from the order.

---

## Resending a ticket

If a client says they didn't receive their ticket:
1. Open the order.
2. Click **Resend confirmation** (or **Resend ticket**).
3. The ticket email is sent again to the client's address.
