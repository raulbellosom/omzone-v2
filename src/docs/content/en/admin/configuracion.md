# Settings

The **Settings** section is where you configure the platform-wide behavior of OMZONE — notifications, branding, cancellation policies, and more.

> Only Administrators have access to Settings. Operators see this section as read-only or not at all.

---

## What you can configure

### Business information
- Business name, logo, and contact details.
- These appear on emails, tickets, and the public site footer.

### Notifications
Configure which automatic emails are sent and when:
- Booking confirmation
- Booking reminder (e.g., 24 hours before the session)
- Cancellation notice
- Pass expiry reminder

For each notification you can customize the message template.

### Cancellation policy
Define the default cancellation and refund policy:
- How many hours in advance a client can cancel.
- Whether refunds are automatic or require manual approval.

### Checkout settings
- Which currencies are accepted.
- Whether clients can pay in installments (if supported by your payment processor).
- Any checkout-level discounts or promo codes.

### Users and access
Manage accounts for your team:
- Create, edit, and deactivate admin and operator accounts.
- See the last time each user logged in.

---

## Changing settings carefully

Some settings affect every new transaction going forward. For example, changing the cancellation policy won't retroactively change anything for orders that already exist — but it will affect all new bookings.

If you're unsure about the impact of a change, make it during off-peak hours and test with a controlled booking first.
