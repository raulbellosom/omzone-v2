# Clients

The **Clients** section is a directory of everyone who has registered or made a purchase through OMZONE.

---

## Finding a client

1. Go to **Clients** in the sidebar.
2. Use the search bar to find by name, email, or phone number.

---

## What's in a client profile

Click any client to open their profile:

- **Personal info** — Name, email, phone.
- **Active passes** — Which passes they currently have and how many sessions remain.
- **Order history** — Every purchase they've made.
- **Active tickets** — Upcoming reservations with QR codes.
- **Activity log** — A timestamped record of notable actions on their account.

---

## Editing a client's info

Administrators can update a client's name and phone number from their profile. Email addresses are used for login and can't be changed from this view — contact the OMZONE support team if a client needs their email updated.

---

## A client's passes and balance

If a client asks "how many sessions do I have left on my pass?", this is where you find out:
1. Open their profile.
2. Go to **Active passes**.
3. You'll see each pass with its remaining balance and expiry date.

---

## Helping a client with a ticket

If a client can't find their ticket or needs it resent:
1. Open their profile.
2. Find the order in question.
3. Open the order and click **Resend confirmation**.

---

## Exporting client data

Use the **Export** button (if available) to download client records. Useful for newsletters, outreach, or CRM tools.
