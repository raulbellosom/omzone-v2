# Assisted sale

**Assisted sale** lets you place a purchase on behalf of a client without them needing to go through the public site. It's for walk-ins, phone orders, and situations where a client needs your help completing their booking.

---

## When to use it

- A client shows up in person and wants to pay cash or via a separate payment method.
- A client calls and asks you to reserve a spot for them.
- A client had trouble completing their purchase online and you need to do it for them.
- You want to register attendance at a session that wasn't booked online.

---

## How to place an assisted sale

1. Go to **Assisted Sale** in the sidebar.
2. Find or create the client:
   - Search by name or email.
   - If they're not in the system, you can create them on the spot.
3. Select the experience.
4. Choose the date and time (from available schedules).
5. Select the price tier.
6. Add any extras (add-ons) if needed.
7. Select the payment method (cash, card, bank transfer, etc.).
8. Confirm the sale.

A ticket is generated immediately and linked to the client's account.

---

## Payment methods in assisted sale

Assisted sale supports payment methods that aren't available on the public site:
- Cash
- Bank transfer
- Card on location
- Courtesy (no charge — for complimentary entries)

The payment method is recorded in the order for your accounting records.

---

## The client receives a ticket

Even though the sale was made from the panel, the client still gets a ticket in their portal (if they have an account) and receives a confirmation email.

---

## No account? No problem

If the client doesn't have an account, you can register the sale under a temporary record. If they later create an account with the same email, their purchase history will be linked.
