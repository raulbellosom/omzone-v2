# Booking requests

A **booking request** is what happens when a client is interested in an experience but doesn't pay immediately — they send an inquiry first, and you respond with a confirmation or a quote.

This flow is used for private events, group experiences, and any experience where you need to align details before accepting payment.

---

## How requests arrive

When an experience is set to **Request** mode (instead of direct purchase), the **Book now** button becomes **Send a request**. The client fills in:
- Their preferred date(s)
- Number of people
- Special requests or questions

The request lands in your panel with status **Pending**.

---

## Reviewing a request

1. Go to **Booking Requests** in the sidebar.
2. Open any **Pending** request.
3. You'll see the client's details, their message, and what they're asking for.

---

## Responding to a request

You have three options:

### Confirm directly
If everything is clear and you can accommodate the request:
- Click **Confirm**.
- The client receives an email with a payment link.
- Once they pay, a ticket is generated.

### Send a quote
If you need to negotiate price or clarify details:
1. Enter the quoted amount in the **Quoted price** field.
2. Write a message to the client.
3. Click **Send quote**.
4. The client receives the quote by email and can accept it and pay directly from the link.

### Decline
If you can't accommodate the request:
1. Click **Decline**.
2. Optionally write a brief explanation for the client.
3. The client receives a notification that their request wasn't accepted.

---

## Best practice

Try to respond to requests within 24 hours. Clients who don't hear back quickly often move on.

---

## Request statuses

| Status    | What it means                                     |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Pending   | Waiting for your response                         |
| Quoted    | You've sent a quote — waiting for client payment  |
| Confirmed | Client paid — ticket generated                    |
| Declined  | Request was turned down                           |
| Expired   | Client didn't respond to the quote in time        |
