# Add-ons

Add-ons are optional extras that clients can include when they book. Think of a massage after a yoga session, a pre-retreat kit, shuttle service, or a private coaching session.

---

## Creating an add-on

1. Go to **Add-ons** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New add-on**.
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Name** — What the add-on is called (e.g., "Post-session massage").
   - **Description** — A short explanation of what it includes.
   - **Price** — What it costs.
   - **Currency** — MXN or USD.
4. Save the add-on.

---

## Attaching an add-on to an experience

Creating an add-on doesn't automatically link it to anything. You need to connect it to one or more experiences:

1. Open the experience you want to add it to.
2. Go to the **Add-ons** section within the experience.
3. Select the add-on(s) you want to offer.
4. Save.

Now clients will see these add-ons as options during checkout.

---

## Add-on availability

Add-ons can be:
- **Optional** — Clients choose whether to include them.
- **Required** — Less common, but possible for experiences where an extra is mandatory.

---

## Managing existing add-ons

From the **Add-ons** list you can:
- Edit the name, description, or price of any add-on.
- Toggle it active or inactive.
- See which experiences it's linked to.

---

## Price changes

Like experience prices, changing an add-on price only affects future bookings. Existing orders keep the price they were charged.
