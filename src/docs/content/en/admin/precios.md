# Pricing

Each experience can have one or more price levels — general admission, member pricing, early bird, group rates, and so on.

---

## Adding a price to an experience

1. Open the experience.
2. Go to the **Pricing** section.
3. Click **Add price**.
4. Fill in the fields:
   - **Name** — What this price tier is called (e.g., "General", "Early Bird").
   - **Amount** — The price number.
   - **Currency** — MXN or USD.
   - **Description** — Optional note about what's included or who it's for.
5. Toggle it **Active**.
6. Save.

---

## Multiple price tiers

You can have as many price tiers as you need. At checkout, clients see all active tiers and choose the one that applies to them.

**Example:**
- General — $800 MXN
- Members — $600 MXN
- Early bird (limited spots) — $650 MXN

---

## Price status

| Status   | What it means                                    |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Active   | Visible at checkout — clients can select it      |
| Inactive | Hidden — clients won't see this price            |

Deactivating a price doesn't affect orders that already used it.

---

## Changing a price

You can edit a price at any time. **The change only affects future purchases** — existing orders always keep the price that was in effect when the client paid.

This is intentional. It means you can update prices freely without worrying about retroactively changing what a client paid.

---

## Removing a price

If you no longer want to offer a price tier, set it to **Inactive** rather than deleting it. This preserves the record for any past orders that used it.

---

## Prices and passes

If you have pass-based pricing, passes are configured separately under **Packages & Passes**. A pass can give access to experiences that also have individual prices — or it can be the only way to book a particular experience (using the **Pass only** sale mode).
