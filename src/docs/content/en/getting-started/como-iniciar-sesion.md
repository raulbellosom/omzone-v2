# How to log in

OMZONE has two separate spaces depending on who you are: the **Admin Panel** and the **Client Portal**. Logging in works the same way for both.

---

## Logging in to the Admin Panel

1. Go to the OMZONE website.
2. Click **Log in** in the navigation bar.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. If you have admin or operator access, you'll be taken to the admin panel automatically.

> The admin panel is at `/admin`. You can bookmark that address for quick access.

---

## Logging in to the Client Portal

1. Go to the OMZONE website.
2. Click **Log in**.
3. Enter your email and password.
4. Clients are taken directly to their portal.

---

## Forgot your password?

1. On the login screen, click **Forgot your password?**
2. Enter your email address.
3. Check your inbox for a link to create a new password.
4. Click the link and follow the steps.

> Check your spam folder if the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

---

## Creating an account

Clients can register directly from the site:

1. Click **Log in** in the navigation bar.
2. Select **Create an account**.
3. Fill in your name, email, and password.
4. Check your inbox and confirm your email if prompted.

> Admin and operator accounts are created by an administrator — they can't be self-registered.

---

## Logging out

Click your name or avatar in the top right corner of the screen and select **Log out**.
