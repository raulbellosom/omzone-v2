# Roles and permissions

OMZONE has three user roles. Each one has a different level of access to the platform.

---

## The three roles

### Administrator

Full access to everything:
- Create, edit, and delete experiences, pricing, schedules, and content
- Manage orders, tickets, and refunds
- View and manage all clients
- Configure platform settings
- Access all reports and activity logs
- Create and manage operator accounts

**This is the owner or lead manager of the business.**

---

### Operator

Day-to-day access without touching sensitive settings:
- View and manage experiences, schedules, and content
- Manage orders and tickets
- Use the assisted sale tool
- View clients (no sensitive data)
- Cannot change platform settings or financial configurations

**This is a staff member who runs the operation daily.**

---

### Client

Access to their own portal only:
- View their tickets and reservations
- Manage their passes
- Review their purchase history
- Update their personal profile

**This is anyone who has bought or reserved an experience.**

---

## What can each role see?

| Feature                   | Administrator | Operator | Client |
| ------------------------- | :-----------: | :------: | :----: |
| Admin panel               | ✓             | ✓        | —      |
| Manage experiences        | ✓             | ✓        | —      |
| Manage pricing            | ✓             | —        | —      |
| Manage orders & tickets   | ✓             | ✓        | —      |
| Assisted sale             | ✓             | ✓        | —      |
| Platform settings         | ✓             | —        | —      |
| Client portal             | —             | —        | ✓      |
| Own tickets & passes      | —             | —        | ✓      |

---

## How do I know which role I have?

Look at the menu available after you log in:
- If you see the full admin panel with all sections → **Administrator**
- If you see the admin panel but some settings are missing → **Operator**
- If you see your personal portal with tickets and passes → **Client**

---

## Can someone have more than one role?

No. Each account has one role. If you need broader access, an administrator can update your account.
