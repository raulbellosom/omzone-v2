# Admin panel tour

When you log in as an administrator or operator, you land on the admin panel. Here's a quick map of what's where.

---

## The sidebar

The left sidebar is your main navigation. It's organized into logical groups:

### Catalog
Everything related to what you sell:
- **Experiences** — Your sessions, retreats, immersions, and stays
- **Pricing** — Price levels for each experience
- **Add-ons** — Optional extras clients can add to their bookings
- **Packages & Passes** — Bundles and membership passes

### Schedule
The operational side:
- **Agenda** — Your full calendar of upcoming sessions
- **Resources** — Instructors, rooms, and equipment
- **Locations** — Physical spaces where experiences happen

### Sales
- **Booking Requests** — Inquiries from clients waiting for a quote or confirmation
- **Orders** — All purchases made on the platform
- **Tickets** — Individual entry tickets for each booking
- **Assisted Sale** — Place an order on behalf of a client directly from the panel

### People
- **Clients** — All registered users who have made a purchase

### Content
- **Publications** — The editorial content that powers your public pages
- **Sections & Blocks** — The building blocks of each publication
- **Media** — Photos and files

### Settings
- **Configuration** — Platform-wide settings (notifications, branding, etc.)
- **Documentation** — This documentation

---

## The top bar

In the top bar you'll find:
- A link back to the public site
- Notifications
- Your account menu (profile, log out)

---

## The dashboard (home)

The first thing you see after logging in is the **Summary** — a quick look at:
- Recent orders
- Upcoming sessions today
- Recent activity

Think of it as your daily briefing.

---

## Tips for getting around

- Use the **sidebar** to navigate between sections.
- Use the **search** (if available) to find a specific client, order, or experience quickly.
- Most sections follow the same pattern: a list view → click an item → detail/edit view.
