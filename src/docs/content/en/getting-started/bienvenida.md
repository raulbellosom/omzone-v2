# Welcome to OMZONE

OMZONE is a platform for booking and managing wellness experiences — yoga sessions, immersions, retreats, private events, and more.

This documentation is for the people who run OMZONE: administrators, operators, and clients. You won't find code here — just clear, step-by-step guides for everything you need to do.

| Operator         | Handle day-to-day content and operations                             |

## What is OMZONE exactly?

OMZONE is built around **experiences** — not rooms, not classes, not appointments. An experience can be a single yoga session, a weekend retreat, a three-night stay, or a private event for a group.

Each experience has its own narrative (the story you tell to attract clients), its own pricing, and its own schedule. The platform handles everything from the moment a visitor lands on the site to the moment they walk in the door.
